3. TRADE-OFF ANALYSIS FOR EDGE DEPLOYMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Feature Dimension Trade-off (512 vs 1024 vs 2048):
   * 512 Features : ~71.41% Accuracy, 68.03% Spam F1. 2.0 KB SRAM. High hash collision rate.
   * 1024 Features: ~72.08% Accuracy, 68.84% Spam F1. 4.0 KB SRAM. Moderate collisions.
   * 2048 Features: ~73.05% Accuracy, 69.52% Spam F1. 8.19 KB SRAM. Low collision rate,
     +1.64% accuracy improvement and +1.49% Spam F1 improvement over 512.

B. Word Analyzer vs. Character N-Grams (char_wb 3-5):
   * Word Analyzer: Fast (< 1.5s transform on 52k texts). Microcontroller tokenization
     loop in C is simple (alphanumeric split + MurmurHash3).
   * Character N-Grams: 6x slower (9.8s). Generates excessive sliding window operations,
     severely degrading microcontroller battery life and real-time processing capability.

C. Regularization & Sparsity (L1 vs L2 vs ElasticNet):
   * L1 Regularization (alpha=1e-4): Drives 83.6% of weights to EXACT zero (only 335 non-zero
     weights out of 2048!). Enables sparse dot-product inference on microcontroller.
   * L2 Regularization: Retains 100% dense non-zero weights (2048 active floats), requiring
     6x more multiplication operations during inference.

D. Loss Function & Class Weighting:
   * loss='log_loss': Outputs well-calibrated posterior probabilities P(spam|x).
   * class_weight='balanced': Prioritizes higher Spam Recall (73.24% vs 62.89% unweighted),
     ensuring malicious spam attacks are not missed.
